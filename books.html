The Girl Under the Rambutan Tree

Nadith wrote this one for his mom, Deepika, who grew up in a little village in Sri Lanka. She sewed her own uniform and spread bananas on her toast to fit in, then learned that true friends love you for who you are. She grew up to be a doctor.
