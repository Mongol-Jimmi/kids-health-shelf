How a book gets made

From a dorm room
to a classroom

Write it

Student authors pitch stories about bodies, feelings and fitting in, then draft them with an editor.

Draw it

Illustrators bring every page to life, so kids from every background can spot themselves.

Print it

Sticker sales and donations pay for real, hold-in-your-hands copies.

Share it

Books land in elementary schools and libraries, free for the kids who read them.

our mission

"Kids' Health Shelf is dedicated to promoting diversity, inclusion, and health education through accessible children's books that empower young readers, reflect diverse lived experiences, and foster compassion, understanding, and belonging in every community."

What we believe
a

Every kid deserves a mirror

Stories should reflect the many different lives kids actually live.

b

Health is more than a body

Feelings, friendships and self-worth are health topics too.

c

Books should be easy to find

We put copies where kids already are: classrooms and libraries.
